Channel 01 · ADS-B · Radio 1090 MHz · from-scratch decoder

Everything overhead, right now

Every aircraft within reception range broadcasts its position twice a second on 1090 MHz. The system is called ADS-B — Automatic Dependent Surveillance–Broadcast — and the station listens and decodes it with a from-scratch decoder. Nothing here is requested; it is simply what arrives.

Positions come straight off the air at the station and are published to a public MQTT broker at wss://live.luisterpost.io, topic luisterpost/aircraft/+/position — connect your own client if you like. Range and bearing are computed in your browser from the station's published coordinates, which are given at city precision. A contact fades once it has not been heard for a minute.
