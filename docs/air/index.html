What the air carries

The fifth medium is the only one made of matter. Sound, radio, light and gamma pass over the station and are gone; dust, pollen and smoke arrive and stay a while.

Not reporting yet

The instrument is built and running — but it is on a bench indoors, and this page will not publish a reading until it is outside in its housing.

That is a deliberate limit rather than an unfinished one. A sensor on a desk measures a room: its temperature, its humidity, whatever is in its air. Presenting that as garden air would be false, and publishing the inside of a house is a different thing from publishing the weather. The station has withdrawn an indoor air channel once already for the same reason.

There is a second reason to wait. The VOC and NOx readings are scored against a baseline the sensor learns over about a day. A baseline learned indoors would travel outside with it and quietly skew the first days of real data. It needs to learn the garden.

Why indices and not concentrations

Metal-oxide gas sensors measure a change in electrical resistance as molecules adsorb onto a heated surface. That change is real and repeatable, but it has no fixed relationship to a concentration in µg/m³ — the response depends on which compounds are present, on humidity, and on the sensor's own history. So the honest output is a relative index against a learned baseline, which is what Sensirion publish and what this page will show.

Absolute NO₂ is achievable, with an electrochemical cell and calibration against a reference station. There is a national monitoring station about a kilometre from here, which makes that a real possibility rather than a wish. It is not what this sensor does.

Where it has to live

The particulate sensor draws air through itself with a small fan, so it cannot be sealed into a weatherproof box — a sealed sensor measures the box. It sits instead in shaded, sheltered ambient air with its inlet facing down, while the electronics live in a separate sealed enclosure above it, because warm air rises and would otherwise drift over the sensor.

Condensation is the real hazard rather than rain. Rain is stopped by pointing the openings downward; condensation forms inside, on cold optics, and degrades the laser path permanently.

Even the housing material matters. Soft plastics and fresh 3D prints outgas volatile compounds — enough that a new enclosure will read as polluted air for a fortnight. Since the VOC index is measured against a learned baseline, a slowly-decaying source like that drags the baseline along with it and hides its own error.

When this channel goes live it will publish on the same public broker as the others, at wss://live.luisterpost.io. Until then it reports only to the station's own logs, and the channel is marked in test in the log on the front page.
